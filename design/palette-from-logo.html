Colours taken from the logo

The logo is one hue family: purple at about 265–275°, from a near-black violet to a pale orchid. Everything below is pulled from it, with black and white as the backgrounds.

1. What the logo actually contains

Dominant colours measured from the logo’s pixels (wordmark and triangle mark together). Click any swatch to copy it.

2. The brand scale

Eleven steps built on those measured colours. Dark steps carry text and pressed states, mid steps carry actions, light steps carry tints and highlights.

3. In use: white and black backgrounds

Same components, two themes. Light uses pure white; dark uses pure black.
